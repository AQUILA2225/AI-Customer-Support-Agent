RETURN POLICY

Customers can return eligible products within 30 days from the date of delivery.

To be eligible for a return, the product must be unused, undamaged, and returned in its original packaging.

Certain items, including personalized products and digital products, are not eligible for return.

Once the returned product is received and inspected, the refund will be processed within 5 to 7 business days.


REFUND POLICY

Refunds are issued only after the returned product has been received and inspected.

The refund will be processed to the original payment method.

Depending on the customer's bank or payment provider, it may take an additional 3 to 5 business days for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's account.

Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the product was damaged or incorrect.


SHIPPING POLICY

Orders are usually processed within 1 to 2 business days.

After processing, shipping typically takes between 3 and 7 business days depending on the delivery location.

Customers will receive tracking information once their order has been shipped.

Delivery dates may be affected by weather conditions, holidays, or other unexpected circumstances.


CANCELLATION POLICY

Customers can request to cancel an order before it has been shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled.

Customers may instead return the product according to the return policy after delivery.

Cancellation requests are subject to order processing status.


PAYMENT POLICY

We accept major credit cards, debit cards, and supported online payment methods.

Payment must be successfully completed before an order is processed.

If a payment fails, the order will not be confirmed.

Customers experiencing payment issues should contact customer support.
